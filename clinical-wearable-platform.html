Clinical wearable platform

A chest-worn platform carrying a sealed reusable module on a disposable adhesive backing, adapted across HRV, SpO₂ and other physiological parameters.

The brief

Reusable module, disposable skin

The electronics had to survive hundreds of wear cycles while the part touching skin was thrown away daily. That split — a sealed reusable module clipping into a low-cost adhesive carrier — set the mechanical architecture, the sealing strategy and the contact design.

Thickness was the other constraint. Anything that stood proud of the chest would catch on clothing, so the module profile was driven down until the battery and the antenna set the floor.

Exploded view

Eight parts, one sealed module

Every layer was tooled in-house: the upper and lower shells, the carrier frame, the board, the flexible display window and the adhesive backing that is replaced at each wear.
