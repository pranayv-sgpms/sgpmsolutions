Women’s health wearable

A soft-band wearable with an over-moulded sensor module, worn overnight and delivered as one end-to-end programme — fabric, electronics, firmware, app and production line.

The brief

Worn every night, noticed by no one

The device had to be worn overnight, every night, without being noticed. That constraint drove every decision — band width, closure, module thickness, the material against skin and where the electronics could physically sit.

We took the programme from first concept sketch to a production line running finished units — fabric selection, tooling, over-moulding, sensor board, firmware, companion app and the pilot build.

Software & intelligence

The app, the model and the prediction

Built alongside the hardware: live SpO₂, temperature and bioimpedance on one dashboard, closed-loop control of the heating element, and history the user can act on.
