Micro Inspect camera system

A slim borescope probe with illumination at the tip and capture, share and brightness control on the handle — for looking inside assemblies that cannot be taken apart. Designed, manufactured and sold by us.

Why we built it

Thin enough to reach, bright enough to see

Two numbers set the whole design: the outside diameter of the probe tip, and how much usable light could be put down a cable that thin. Everything else — sensor choice, cable stiffness, handle ergonomics — followed from those.

Controls sit on the handle rather than in software, so an inspector working one-handed inside a machine can capture and share a frame without looking away from the screen.

Applications

Where it earns its place

Anywhere a fault has to be found without disassembly — turbine hot sections, riveted airframe panels, bearing housings, populated boards, pipe bores and precision assemblies.
