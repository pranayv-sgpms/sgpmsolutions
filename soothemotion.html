A FEW MORE ANSWERS

Good questions.

Why pay 2–3 months before delivery?

Early access helps fund and plan the first production run. In exchange, confirmed early orders receive the ₹3,999 offer. The working prototype is complete, but production preparation, assembly and final checks take time. If you prefer to see the finished production unit first, join the free waitlist and decide later.

Can I use massage and scratching together?

Yes. SootheMotion supports both together, with separate intensity controls. Final intensity ranges and app compatibility will be published before ordering.

Is ₹8,999 the final retail price?

No. Around ₹8,999 is an indication only; the later price is to be decided. It is not an established selling price or the basis for a percentage-discount claim.

Does the waitlist reserve one of the 500 devices?

No. It records interest and lets us send development and ordering updates. A unit and the early-access price are confirmed only through the ordering process once paid orders open.

What if January dispatch is delayed?

We will share the revised estimate when a delay becomes known. You may cancel for a full refund before dispatch. Courier delivery time after dispatch is separate.

Are these photos of the working prototype?

No. The working prototype is complete, but these images are visualisations based on the current design. The app screens and packaging are concepts. A prototype demonstration video will be added when available.

How will I receive updates?

The waitlist collects your name and email, with an optional city, for SootheMotion development and ordering updates. You can unsubscribe by emailing info@sgpmsolutions.com. See our Privacy Policy for details.
