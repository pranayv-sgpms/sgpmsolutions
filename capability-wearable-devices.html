Customized wearable devices

Body-worn devices built to partner specification — fabric wearables, soft bands, patches and rigid modules, with the sensing, power and connectivity designed for the place on the body they have to sit.

01 Formats 02 Inside the device 03 Constraints
01 · Formats

What we build

The format follows the parameter being measured and the wear duration, not the other way round.

Soft-band wearables Chest, waist, wrist and limb bands
Adhesive patches Reusable module on disposable carrier
Garment-integrated Sensors built into the fabric
Rigid modules Clip-on and dock-mounted units
Handheld devices Spot-check recorders
Charging & docks Cradles and contact charging
02 · Inside the device

What goes in

Enclosure, board, power and optics are developed together so nothing has to be re-laid out late.

Sensor front end Chosen for the signal, not the datasheet
Low-power MCU Duty-cycled for multi-day wear
Radio BLE, with WiFi where the use case needs it
Power Cell sizing, charging and protection
Enclosure Over-moulded, sealed and skin-safe
Firmware & app Acquisition through to companion app
03 · Constraints

What decides the design

Wearables are won or lost on the constraints that are set first.

Wear duration Overnight, multi-day or continuous
Placement Where the signal is, not where it is easy
Thickness Profile driven down until power sets the floor
Skin contact Biocompatibility and irritation
Wash & sweat Ingress and cleaning over the full life
Cost per wear Reusable versus disposable split
